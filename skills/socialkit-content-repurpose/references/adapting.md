# Adapting one source for several platforms

- Find the single most useful idea in the source; each platform post carries one idea.
- Facebook: a short paragraph or two; a link post when the source is online.
- Instagram: lead with the visual; the caption supports it. Links are not clickable, so
  point to the bio or say where to find it.
- Threads: conversational, under 500 characters; a question or a clear takeaway.
- Video sources: quote timestamps or lines accurately; do not paraphrase into new claims.
- Keep numbers, names and quotes exactly as in the source. If the source lacks a number
  a platform post would need, leave it out or ask.
- Disclose sponsorships and AI-generated media on every platform where the source has them.
