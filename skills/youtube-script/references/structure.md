# Script structure

## Long-form (4-20 minutes)

1. Hook (0-15 s): the promise and why it matters now. No channel intro first.
2. Context (15-45 s): what the viewer needs to follow along.
3. Body: 3-6 sections, each with one clear point, an example and a transition.
4. Payoff: deliver the promise from the hook explicitly.
5. Call to action: one action, specific, tied to the content.

## Shorts (up to 3 minutes, vertical 1080x1920)

1. Hook in the first 1-2 seconds, on screen and spoken.
2. One idea only.
3. End on the payoff; a loopable ending helps retention.

## Writing for voice

- Read it out loud. Cut anything you stumble on.
- Numbers: say them the way you would speak them.
- Avoid long lists in narration; show them on screen instead.
