# Voice rules (defaults)

These apply when the user has no voice file, and as a baseline under one.

## Do

- Lead with the most useful or surprising point.
- Use the words the audience uses. Define jargon once or avoid it.
- Prefer numbers, names and examples to adjectives.
- Keep sentences short. One idea per sentence.
- Write calls to action that say exactly what to do and why.

## Avoid

- Emojis (unless enabled by the voice file).
- Hype words: revolutionary, game-changing, insane, mind-blowing, ultimate, secret,
  unbelievable, epic, crushing it, next level, must-watch.
- Fake urgency: "before it is too late", "they do not want you to know", countdowns that
  are not real.
- Clickbait the content does not deliver. A title or thumbnail must describe what the
  viewer actually gets.
- Invented statistics, quotes, testimonials or results. If a number is not in the
  source material, ask for it or leave it out.
- Engagement bait: "comment YES", "tag three friends", like-for-like requests.
- Hashtag stuffing. Use a few relevant tags only where the platform uses them.

## Honesty and disclosure

- Sponsorships, gifted products and affiliate links must be disclosed in the content
  itself, in the platform's native disclosure field where one exists, or both.
- Realistic AI-generated or altered people, places or events must be disclosed. On
  YouTube use the synthetic media flag at upload.
- Do not imitate a real person's voice, likeness or brand without their permission.

## Accessibility

- Provide captions or a transcript for video when possible.
- Describe images in alt text where the platform supports it.
- Do not put essential information only in an image or only in audio.
